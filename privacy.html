Curio

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-13 · Applies to the Curio Chrome extension

The short version: Curio has no backend of its own. Your chapter content and chat live only in this browser's memory while the window is open, and are gone the moment you close it. The only place your content is ever sent is the AI provider you (a parent or teacher) choose and connect with your own API key.

1. What Curio is

Curio is a browser extension that turns a photographed, scanned, or typed chapter into a private, age-appropriate AI tutor for that one chapter. It runs entirely in your browser — there is no Curio server, account system, or database.

Nothing in the table above is ever sent to a server operated by us, because we do not operate one.

3. What your chosen AI provider sees

When you upload a chapter or ask a question, Curio sends the relevant chapter content and your question directly from your browser to the AI provider you selected in Settings — currently OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google Gemini. We do not see or intercept this traffic; it goes straight from your browser to their API using the key you provided.

Please review your chosen provider's own privacy policy and terms, since their data retention and training practices are set by them, not us:

  • OpenAI — Privacy Policy · Terms
  • Anthropic — Privacy Policy · Terms
  • Google Gemini — Privacy Policy · Terms

Where a provider offers a no-training / zero-retention option for API traffic, we recommend it, especially for younger students.

4. One more first-run download

To search your chapter privately, Curio runs a small local "embedding" model in your browser. Its weights are downloaded once from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) and cached by your browser — this download contains no data about you or your chapter, only the model file itself.

5. Children's privacy

Curio is designed to be set up by a parent or teacher on behalf of a child. The adult provides the AI provider account and API key, sees a plain-language disclosure of what that provider will process, and explicitly consents before the child can use the tutor. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children ourselves — we have no accounts and no server. The child's chapter content and questions are only processed by the AI provider the adult selected, under that adult's account and consent.

If you are a parent or teacher, please review the linked provider terms above — some providers set their own age or consent requirements for API use.

6. Security

  • All communication with your AI provider happens over HTTPS.
  • Your API key is stored only in chrome.storage.local, never synced across devices, and never logged.
  • The extension requests access only to the specific AI provider domains and the model-weights host — nothing broader.
  • No remote code execution: the extension runs no dynamically fetched scripts.

7. Your controls

  • Clear key (Settings) — deletes your stored API key immediately.
  • Clear chapter (Settings) — ends the current session and returns to Upload.
  • Close the window — immediately discards all chapter content and chat history.
  • Remove the extension — deletes all locally stored settings and progress records.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Since Curio has no account system, we cannot notify you directly — please check back if you have concerns.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the extension's listed support contact on its Chrome Web Store page.
