Setup (parents & teachers)

Curio needs an AI provider API key to work. This is a one-time setup step for an adult — the student never sees or enters the key.

1
Open Curio and choose an AI provider: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, or OpenRouter.
2
Paste an API key from that provider (each has a "get a key" link in the setup screen). Choosing OpenRouter allows you to consolidate OpenAI, Claude, and Google models under a single account.
3
Set your child's age and language — Curio matches its vocabulary to this.
4
Confirm the consent checkbox, which discloses that your chosen provider will process the chapter content and questions.

You can change the provider, model, age, or language at any time from the gear icon in Settings.

Uploading a chapter

  • Accepted files: .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp, .pdf, .docx, .txt.
  • Up to 10 files and 25 MB total per chapter.
  • For photos: good lighting and a flat, in-focus page work best. A blurry photo will be flagged for re-upload rather than silently guessed at.
  • PDFs are split into individual pages automatically.

If your upload has more than one chapter

Curio works best focused on a single chapter. If it detects your upload spans more than one chapter or section, it will ask which one you want to study before building the tutor — or you can choose "use the whole upload" if the split doesn't look right.

Chatting — and math

Ask anything about the uploaded chapter in plain language. Curio answers only from what you uploaded, and will say so plainly if something isn't covered rather than guessing.

Math & science problems

Curio can work through calculations step by step, showing each step and the rule behind it, with equations rendered properly (including chemistry formulas). It also double-checks its own arithmetic — if a calculation doesn't add up, you'll see a small "double-check this" note under the answer.

Puzzles & the final quiz

  • Quick checks: every five questions, Curio pauses with a short multiple-choice, fill-in-the-blank, or true/false check. You can skip any puzzle.
  • "Test me": click this any time in chat for a graded end-of-chapter quiz — a mix of multiple-choice and short written questions, scored out of 100 with a summary and specific "topics to review". Curio remembers what you found tricky and revisits it next time you study the same chapter.

Chapter Library

Curio maintains a local-encrypted library of your recently uploaded chapters (up to 5 chapters at a time) directly on the main upload screen.

  • Instant Load: Clicking any chapter card in the list immediately loads its full vectors and chat history, allowing you to switch chapters without re-uploading.
  • Rename: Click the settings (pencil) icon to rename a chapter directly inline.
  • Delete: Click the trash icon to permanently remove the chapter's vector database and study session history from disk.

Performance Dashboard

You can track your learning statistics and quiz progression gains over time inside the Performance Dashboard, accessible via the link next to recent chapters on the upload screen.

  • Progression Curves: Every chapter you take a quiz on renders a custom line graph showing your scores over multiple attempts, allowing you to visualize your improvement curve.
  • Review Lists: Displays a list of active weak topics you struggled with in recent assessments, helping you focus your review.
  • Encrypted storage: All progression logs and scores are securely encrypted on your local device. You can clear them at any time using the "Clear Assessment History" button.

Settings

  • Age — controls vocabulary and sentence length.
  • Language — chat and puzzles are written in this language.
  • AI provider & model — one model handles both reading photos and answering; newer models generally do better with harder material.
  • API key — paste a new key to replace the current one, or clear it entirely.
  • Clear chapter — ends the session, deletes the active session state, and returns to Upload.

Troubleshooting

"Your API key looks invalid or expired"Double-check the key was copied in full with no extra spaces, and that it's active on your provider's dashboard. Paste a fresh key in Settings.
"The tutor is resting" / rate-limit messagesYour provider is temporarily limiting requests. Wait a minute and try again — Curio already retries automatically before showing this.
"Couldn't read this page clearly"The photo was too blurry, dark, or low-detail to read reliably. Retake it in better light, holding the camera steady and the page flat.
"That's not in this chapter"This is expected — Curio only answers from what you uploaded. Ask about something the chapter actually covers, or upload more pages.
A math answer looks wrongIf Curio itself flags "double-check the arithmetic", it already suspects a mistake in its own working — verify that step by hand and ask it to redo it.
Active Session ResiliencyYour chapter and chat are automatically saved encrypted on your device. If you close the window, your active session is restored. If you wish to delete it permanently, click the Discard button in the chat header.

Privacy

Curio stores your chapter content and chat history fully encrypted on your local disk. See the full Privacy Policy for details on what's stored locally and what your AI provider sees.
